Six detectors. One ranked answer.

Every detector explains itself in plain English — a score plus the reason it fired, so you can trust the ranking or dig into the why.

⚡

Breakout

Close clears the prior 20-day high on elevated volume — momentum with participation behind it.

🎯

Oversold bounce

RSI dips under 30, then turns up alongside price. Catches the recovery, not the falling knife.

✂️

MACD cross

Momentum flips as MACD crosses its signal line — extra weight when it happens below zero, early.

✨

Golden cross

The 50-day average crosses above the 200-day. Slow to fire, hard to fake.

📊

Volume spike

Volume z-score ≥ 2.5 with a directional close. Someone big just showed their hand.

🌀

Bollinger squeeze

Bandwidth in the bottom decile of 120 days — volatility coiling before it picks a direction.
