# Shark Adaptive Research Engine — Implementation Map

1. Foundation — repository, dashboard, configuration, CI, AI adapter.
2. Quant research — OHLCV models, providers, SMC/candles, hypotheses, validation.
3. Multi-market intelligence — cross-market features and universe expansion.
4. MAG7/VIX regime engine — regime classification and contextual features.
5. Adaptive discovery — feature discovery and falsification.
6. Strategy evolution — model generations, registry, graveyard and ranking.
7. Agent orchestra — research, SMC, candle, intermarket, regime, quant, falsification, audit, risk and reporting roles.
8. Autonomous daily research — scheduled cycle and report generation.
9. Model registry — persistent model evidence and promotion states.
10. Production signal engine — validated research outputs only.
11. Paper trading — forward validation ledger.
12. Risk engine — hard limits and exposure controls.
13. Live execution — future adapter, disabled by default.
14. Continuous self-audit — regression, data quality, model drift and research refresh.

Important: a phase being represented in code does not mean it is statistically validated. Production eligibility requires evidence from real datasets and forward testing.
